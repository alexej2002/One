One: Daily Motivation

Short description
Daily motivation and inspiring quotes. One meaningful thought every day.

Full description

One is a simple daily motivation app built around one idea: one meaningful quote every day.

Start your day with a motivational quote for inspiration, reflection and a positive mindset. No endless feed. No social network. No distractions. Just one thought for your day.

DAILY MOTIVATION

Discover one inspiring quote every day. Take a moment to pause, reflect and carry a meaningful thought with you.

MOTIVATIONAL & INSPIRATIONAL QUOTES

Discover quotes about life, growth, courage, focus, change and moving forward. One keeps the experience simple so the words have room to matter.

YOUR DAILY QUOTE

Choose when you want to receive your daily reminder. Morning, afternoon or evening, One can become a quiet daily ritual without adding another endless stream of content to your day.

SAVE YOUR FAVORITE QUOTES

Keep the motivational quotes and thoughts that matter to you in Favorites and return to them whenever you need inspiration.

YOUR QUOTE ARCHIVE

Revisit previous daily quotes and rediscover thoughts from earlier days with ONE+.

BEAUTIFUL THEMES

Enjoy carefully designed themes with calm backgrounds, typography and colors that keep your daily quote at the center of the experience.

ONE+

Unlock more of One with additional themes, your quote archive, Favorites, home screen widgets and extended reminder options.

One can also be used for free.

WHY ONE?

Most apps give you more content. One deliberately gives you less.

One quote. One day. One moment to think.

Whether you're looking for daily motivation, motivational quotes, inspirational quotes, a positive thought or a simple quote of the day, One keeps it simple.

One. One day. One thought.
