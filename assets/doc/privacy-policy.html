ONE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the One mobile application ("One", "we", "us", or "our") accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app.

Privacy summary.
One is designed to work without a user account, advertising, analytics, or a One-operated backend. Based on the current version of the app, One does not collect or share personal or sensitive user data through a One-operated server.

1. Information One accesses or collects

One does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, messages, health information, or other profile information.

The current version of One does not intentionally collect personal or sensitive user data through a One-operated server.

2. Information stored locally on your device

To provide app functionality, One may store certain information locally on your device. This may include:

  • selected appearance and theme settings;
  • favorite quotes;
  • quote archive or history information;
  • notification and reminder preferences; and
  • local entitlement or interface state used by the app.

This information is used only to provide app functionality and, in the current app design, is not transmitted to a One-operated server.

3. Data sharing

One does not sell personal or sensitive user data.

One does not intentionally share locally stored quote activity, favorites, archive information, or app preferences with third parties.

4. Google Play and purchases

ONE+ purchases and subscriptions are offered through Google Play. Google Play may process information necessary to complete purchases, manage subscriptions, verify purchase status, prevent fraud, and provide Google Play services.

One does not receive or store your full payment-card details. Information processed directly by Google is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

5. Notifications

If you enable reminders, One may use notification functionality provided by Android. Notification preferences may be stored locally on your device. You can disable notifications at any time through Android settings.

6. Third-party SDKs and services

The current version of One does not intentionally include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs.

Google Play services involved in distributing the app and processing purchases operate under Google's own terms and privacy practices.

7. Security

Because One does not maintain user accounts or a One-operated database of app activity, the amount of user information handled by One is intentionally limited.

Locally stored app information is subject to the security protections provided by Android and your device. Google Play transaction information is handled through Google Play's systems.

8. Data retention

One does not maintain a One-operated server-side database containing your quote activity, favorites, archive, or profile information.

Local app information generally remains on your device until you remove it, clear the app's storage, reset the relevant setting, or uninstall the app, subject to Android and device behavior.

Google may retain purchase and transaction records according to Google's own retention practices and applicable law.

9. Data deletion

One does not provide user accounts, so there is no One account to delete.

You can generally remove locally stored One data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling One. If a future version of One introduces account creation or server-side personal data, this Privacy Policy and the app's deletion mechanisms will be updated accordingly.

10. Children's privacy

One does not knowingly request personal information from children through the app. The current app does not provide user accounts, social features, advertising, or user-generated content.

11. International users

One may be made available in multiple countries. Nothing in this Privacy Policy is intended to limit privacy rights that cannot lawfully be waived under applicable law.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if One's features, data practices, third-party services, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
